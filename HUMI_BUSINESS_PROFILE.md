## 1. Executive Summary

**HuMi** is a Capital Management Operating System that puts institutional-grade trading tools in the pocket of every African trader. We operate as an API connector and technology mediator between brokers and users — not a broker ourselves.

**Core Promise:**  
*"HuMi puts institutional-grade trading tools in the pocket of every African trader."*

**What we are:** A mobile-first gateway to professional trading tools — connecting MT4, MT5, Deriv, and crypto wallets into one unified dashboard with AI assistance, copy trading, and instant cross-broker transfers.

**What we are NOT:** A broker, financial advisor, or fund manager. Our broker partners hold the necessary FSCA licences; HuMi is a technology connector.

## 5. Target Customer Personas

### A. Mobile-First Traders
- **Age:** 25–40
- **Location:** Urban South Africa
- **Experience:** 1–3 years active trading on phone
- **Pain:** Desktop platforms don't work on mobile; miss trades while away from laptop; juggle multiple broker apps.

### B. Signal & Copy Trading Enthusiasts
- Follow signals on WhatsApp/Telegram, want automation and verification.
- **Pain:** Unregulated signal groups, no performance verification.

### C. Multi-Account Traders
- Users with 2+ broker accounts wanting a unified dashboard.
- **Pain:** Manual tracking, slow/expensive transfers between brokers.

### D. Beginners (Training Center)
- New traders seeking education and a safe starting point.
- **Pain:** Overwhelmed by information, expensive courses with no practical application.

### Top 3 User Objections
1. "Too expensive" → Free Starter tier + 14-day money-back guarantee.
2. "Don't trust AI with my trades" → Transparent track record, education on how Khumo works.
3. "I already have a broker" → HuMi connects to existing accounts — it's an enhancement, not a replacement.

### What Users Praise Most
- Connect multiple accounts in one place
- One-click trading signals
- Profitable copy trading and AI bot execution
- Mobile-first design

4. **Premium Add-ons** — AI bot access, priority support, advanced analytics.
5. **B2B Licensing** — White-label for institutions (future).

### Unit Economics (Estimated)
| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| MetaAPI per account | ~$2/mo |
| Average accounts per user | 2–3 |
| Supabase + AI per user | ~$5/mo |
| **Total cost per user** | **~R160–R200/mo** |
| CAC (current) | ~$2 (~R36) |

### Profitability
Currently burning cash at early stage. Model becomes profitable at scale — Basic tier breaks even at ~R200 cost, Pro/Enterprise tiers are profitable per user.
